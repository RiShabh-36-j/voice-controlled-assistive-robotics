Explainable NLP & Intent Matching

Rather than an opaque black box, the system employs transparent multi-metric classification combining canonical phrase Levenshtein similarity (45%), keyword anchor coverage (40%), and Jaccard token overlap (15%). If confidence is below the safety threshold, execution is halted and clarification requested.

Autonomous Path Planning (A*)

The 960×600 canvas is discretized into a 48×30 occupancy grid with a 26px obstacle inflation zone matching the robot's physical radius. 8-directional search generates optimal, corner-safe trajectories smoothed via line-of-sight raycasting.

Simulated 360° LIDAR Sensor

Casts 36 laser rays in real-time across the environment. Intersections against perimeter boundaries and furniture calculate clearances. The forward ±35° safety cone triggers automatic braking if obstacles breach 35px proximity.

Assistive Healthcare Missions

Supports multi-stage tasks including Patient Assistance (direct bedside transit), Pharmacy Medicine Delivery (two-leg route: dock → pharmacy pickup → patient bedside handover), and Autonomous Docking (inductive recharging bay).

Supported Voice Command Lexicon & Grammar

Canonical voice commands, natural language variations, and associated robot actions recognized by the system.

Move Forward MOVE_FORWARD

Drives the assistive robot forward along its current heading.

“Move forward” “Go forward” “Go ahead” “Move ahead” “Advance” “Drive front”
Move Backward MOVE_BACKWARD

Reverses the assistive robot backward.

“Move backward” “Go backward” “Reverse” “Go back” “Retreat”
Turn Left TURN_LEFT

Rotates robot counter-clockwise (45-90 degrees).

“Turn left” “Rotate left” “Spin left” “Go left”
Turn Right TURN_RIGHT

Rotates robot clockwise (45-90 degrees).

“Turn right” “Rotate right” “Spin right” “Go right”
Stop / Emergency Halt STOP

Instantly brakes all motors and halts active navigation missions.

“Stop” “Please stop” “Emergency stop” “Halt” “Freeze” “Brake”
Move to the Patient NAVIGATE_PATIENT

Navigates autonomously around walls to the patient bedside ward.

“Move to the patient” “Go to patient” “Assist patient” “Visit patient” “Go to bed”
Bring Medicine DELIVER_MEDICINE

“Bring medicine” “Get medicine” “Fetch medicine” “Deliver pills” “Bring medication”

“Help me” “Emergency” “I need help” “SOS” “Urgent help”
